End User License Agreement & Privacy Disclaimer:

1. Privacy & Local P2P Architecture: NearMitra is built on direct peer-to-peer local socket and encrypted WebRTC mesh networking. Your data, files, photos, folders, audio calls, and CCTV feeds are transmitted directly between your devices without any intermediary storage.

2. Cloud Mesh & Firebase Presence: When Internet / WAN mode is activated, minimal device metadata (Device Name, Avatar Emoji, Online Status) is used solely for peer discovery and WebRTC signaling. WebRTC streams use encrypted transport.

3. Windows Integration: The application creates shortcuts in your Windows Start Menu and Desktop, and optionally configures private-network access with your Windows administrator approval.
